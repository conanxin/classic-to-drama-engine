# 《归途：奥德修斯》V2 Character and Cast Matrix

Status: `LOCKED_V2_CAST_STRATEGY`

## Count authority

- Principal cast identities: `4`.
- Nonprincipal credited identities after disguise/voice normalization: `65`.
- Featured supporting roles within those 65: `28`.
- Remaining day-player, voice, creature-performance, and group identities: `37`.
- Core crowd ensemble: `28`, reused across isolated story blocks with separate fittings.

Disguises of Athena and Odysseus are not counted as new cast identities. The actor may be supplemented by a body double only for a transformation match, never to change who performs the choice.

| Character | Episodes / block | Performance track | Casting requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Odysseus | 05–17, 19–30; voice/frame in 08–15 | exposed cleverness → concealed identity → accountable name | precise physical actor; credible sailor/laborer; can underplay wit and carry action continuity |
| Penelope | 01–02, 04, 18, 20–30 | household operator → evidence controller → final human verifier | authority without oracular stillness; hands comfortable with loom, ledgers, keys, and spatial command |

## Day-player, voice, group, and creature identities

The remaining 37 identities include Euryalus, Echeneus, Egyptius, Leocritus, Leodes, Iros, Theoclymenus’ minor counterparts, Phaeacian captain, scout/sailor variants, Helios and Zeus voices, Ino, Siren voices, Scylla partial performer, Laestrygonian group, other Cyclopes voices, suitor/dead/kin groups, farm helpers, and Ithaca civic witnesses. Their scenes are clustered by set and costume state rather than spread as isolated calls.

## Scheduling safeguards

1. Schedule Odysseus salt/wound/makeup states in progression inside each set block.
2. Penelope’s loom/keys/bow/bed scenes receive one continuity rehearsal with the property master.
3. Telemachus’ speech maturation is tracked by episode, even when production shoots out of order.
4. Athena disguise work shares the same pin and eye-line rule but receives separate dialect and body notes.
5. Eurylochus and the six Scylla victims must be cast early enough for ship blocking and named casualty continuity.
6. Hall battle foreground suitors remain consistent from contest through death/cleanup evidence.

Cast result: `4 principal + 28 featured supporting + 37 day/voice/group identities`, with 28-person controlled ensemble reuse.
