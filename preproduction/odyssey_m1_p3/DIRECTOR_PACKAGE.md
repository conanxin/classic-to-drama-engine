## Package purpose

This package turns the frozen V2 screenplay into a common directing, cinematography, editorial, performance and physical-production language. It does not create Screenplay V3. If blocking, camera, set, VFX or scheduling cannot solve a script problem, it is logged in `SCRIPT_CHANGE_REQUESTS.md` without changing V2.

## Non-negotiable dramatic locks

- Gods change conditions; they do not choose for humans.
- Penelope is an active household operator and the final human verifier.
- Telemachus’ authority grows through tasks, failed speech, travel, evidence, secrecy, action and restraint—not a sudden heroic voice.
- Odysseus’ cleverness is not a line-delivery trick. Its cost is exposed when he claims his name and controlled when he accepts another person’s verification.
- Recognition proceeds: name → story/clothes → Argos → scar → bow/axes → bed → father/land → community.
- EP26–28 geography, ammunition, doors, bodies, wounds and weapon custody remain legible.
- EP29 private recognition and EP30 civic closure are not epilogues to rush after the fight.

## Technical posture

- 831 planned shots across 150 scenes; average 5.54 per scene.
- No unresolved over- or under-covered scene under the frozen coverage classes.
- 60 MUST-storyboard scenes; all MUST shots have frame specifications.
- Five standing sets, twelve production units and medium-cost practical-first VFX grammar.
- No full-CG hero creature and no uncontrolled open-water principal performance required.

## Decision rights

- Director owns dramatic purpose, blocking and performance continuity.
- DP owns lighting implementation and camera package within the frozen narrative lens/movement classes.
- 1st AD owns floor lock, company flow, daily feasibility and safety calls.
- Stunt coordinator/armorer owns weapon state and performer release; VFX supervisor owns technical-plate sufficiency.
- Line producer owns cost/schedule alternatives but cannot delete protected recognition or closure beats.
- Script supervisor owns S1 state, prop/wound/knowledge continuity and records any unresolved script request.
