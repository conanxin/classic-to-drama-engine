## S1 custody clocks

- **Bow:** locked through EP23 → Penelope opens and presents EP25 → suitors attempt without stringing success → Telemachus controls handoff → Odysseus strings and retains through battle → property master logs de-string/clean placement after EP28.
- **Axes:** AX01–12 remain on the frozen floor coordinates from first contest setup through the EP26 pass. Battle furniture may occlude but never teleport them.
- **Armory:** weapons removed from hall in EP22; A0 access appears secured; G0 remains the overlooked route; enemy load from Melanthius is visible; recovery closes G0 before final defense.
- **Arrows:** `12 contest → recovered test → 11 battle → 10 → 9 → 5 → 3 → 0`. Each firing/recovery/cutaway is reconciled against `EP26_EP28_ACTION_PREVIS.json`.
- **Bodies/furniture/blood:** their positions are production continuity assets even when not hand props. Nothing is cleared merely to create a camera lane.

## Duplication and release

Every hero/stunt/rubber duplicate is dimensioned and photographed beside the hero. Safe units carry invisible production marks and never appear in recognition inserts. Property release requires incoming/outgoing scene, custodian, state, quantity and photograph. Recognition objects require joint script-supervisor/property sign-off at call and wrap.
