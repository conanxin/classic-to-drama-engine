## S1 and battle continuity

- Suitor wardrobe IDs are bound to table position, contest order and battle outcome. `SU-01` through `SU-21` receive clean, first-blood, full-battle, corpse/withdrawn and aftermath photographs.
- Blood layer progression: `B0 clean`, `B1 Antinous impact`, `B2 first exchanges`, `B3 armory reversal`, `B4 full battle`, `B5 sulfur aftermath`. Layer regression is prohibited inside story order.
- Stunt/rubber weapon harnesses use the same visible tie points as hero units. A wardrobe duplicate is not approved until draw, fall and table-vault clearance pass.
- EP29 cleaning removes loose blood, not seam stains, damage or floor-contact transfer. Recognition is not photographed in a false pristine palace.

## Ensemble separation

Ithaca civic uses earth/undyed vertical layers; Suitors use wine/rust and soft interior wealth; Phaeacia uses sea-glass lateral drape; Sailors use ship-coded shoulder tabs and salt wear; Underworld suppresses color with death-specific edges; Kin/Civic uses ash/black family marks moving toward undyed white. A garment cannot cross pools without a new ID, full refit and release-gap check.

## Department workflow

1. Fit principals in story-state order, then photograph production-order transitions.
2. Each call sheet carries incoming/outgoing wardrobe state and the prior/next scene reference.
3. Wet, blood, fire/smoke and stunt states have at least one matched duplicate; hero recognition garments have one protected pristine archival unit.
4. The script supervisor, costume supervisor and HMU key jointly sign EP16, EP19, EP23, EP26–29 and EP30 transitions.
5. No repair, laundering or distressing is performed from memory. The state ledger and calibrated photographs are the authority.
