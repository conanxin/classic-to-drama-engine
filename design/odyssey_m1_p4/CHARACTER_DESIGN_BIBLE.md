# Character Design Bible

Status: `FROZEN_P4_CHARACTER_VISUAL_SYSTEM`

All faces are fictional casting archetypes. No real actor or celebrity likeness is used. Identity continuity depends on silhouette, proportions, hairline, weathering map, movement and material custody—not a generated face alone.

## Principals

### Odysseus — compact force learning to withhold it

- Silhouette: broad back, rope-work forearms, slightly forward sea stance; right shoulder drops after long oar work.
- Face/hair: dark curled hair broken with salt-grey at temples; irregular short beard; healed brow nick and fixed boar-scar body map.
- Movement: searches exits before faces; touches tools by balance point; storyteller opens stance, beggar reduces occupied space, bow state becomes still.
- Materials: salt-stiff linen, repaired leather, olive wood, borrowed Phaeacian weave, controlled beggar patches.
- States: `ODY-A` war-return memory; `ODY-B` sea-worn; `ODY-C` Phaeacian recovery; `ODY-D` storyteller; `ODY-E` Ithaca beggar; `ODY-F` scar recognition; `ODY-G` bow reclaimed; `ODY-H` battle; `ODY-I` husband; `ODY-J` father/civic return.
- Identity invariant: same asymmetric brow, left-temple grey break, shoulder slope, scar placement and thumb-on-grip habit in all states.

### Penelope — authority through work, distance and delayed verdict

- Silhouette: vertical but never ceremonial; key belt and cloth work create weighted left side; sleeves clear loom and ledger work.
- Face/hair: controlled coils with stress release at temples; sleep loss accumulates without glamourized grief.
- Movement: moves objects before people; stops others by occupying routes; listens while hands continue work, then stops the hand when evidence changes.
- Materials: old purple thread, undyed wool, bronze keys, wax tablets, loom wood.
- States: `PEN-A` household administrator; `PEN-B` public pressure; `PEN-C` private strategist; `PEN-D` contest controller; `PEN-E` verifier; `PEN-F` recognized partner; `PEN-G` restored-not-reset.
- Identity invariant: high asymmetric brow, left-hand key/cloth habit, square stillness when judging; no passive queen tableau.

### Telemachus — authority grows through fit and custody

- Silhouette: early inherited layers are too broad in the shoulder; travel compacts them; returning heir carries sword without checking it; civic state fits without armor display.
- Face/hair: younger open face, hair tied inconsistently before travel and self-kept afterward.
- Movement: begins at edges, touches belt under pressure, learns to choose distance, later blocks routes and finally cuts his own staff.
- States: `TEL-A` boy in household; `TEL-B` uncertain speaker; `TEL-C` traveler; `TEL-D` testimony judge; `TEL-E` returning heir; `TEL-F` secret keeper; `TEL-G` battle heir; `TEL-H` civic authority.
- Identity invariant: long neck, left-foot lead, inherited shoulder seam and sword/scabbard relation remain traceable.

### Athena — condition before spectacle

- Silhouette: every disguise shares a small owl-pin geometry, one slightly forward shoulder and an exact half-turn pause.
- Human states: `ATH-MENTES`, `ATH-MENTOR`, `ATH-GIRL`, `ATH-COMPANION`, `ATH-BOY`, `ATH-SHEPHERD` use locally credible cloth and work clearance.
- `ATH-ALMOST`: crowd or cloth timing breaks, reflected eye-light arrives early, shadow disagrees with practical source.
- `ATH-DIVINE`: structural pale bronze and undyed planes; restrained brightness; no continuous glow or fantasy armor.
- Identity invariant: pin, gait cadence, eye-light position and incorrectly timed shadow.

## Supporting silhouette families

| Character | Silhouette / material signature | Movement and distance rule |
|---|---|---|
| Antinous | broad soft wealth, wine-rust drape, heavy ring hand | occupies Odysseus' seat and lets servants cross distance |
| Eurymachus | narrow tailored layers, polished clasp, forward chin | closes conversational distance while preserving an exit |
| Amphinomus | working shoulder under suitor cloth, muted rust | pauses at shared labor objects; hesitates before crowd motion |
| Eumaeus | repaired fiber, animal-weathered cloak, low tool belt | offers useful objects before trust; holds gates with body |
| Eurycleia | compact wrapped sleeves, basin and key practicality | touch reads body history; work stops only at recognition |
| Nausicaa | laundry-balanced stance, sea-glass repair colors | preserves public distance and gives routes, not rescue poses |
| Alcinous | lateral court drape, cedar staff, maintained sandals | hospitality framed as an accountable public procedure |
| Circe | poison-green edge, clay-red cup, precise bare hands | controls space by arranging others; anger becomes exact stillness |
| Calypso | weather-soft layers that never show labor damage | island control is proximity and endless repair, not seduction glamour |
| Polyphemus | partial scale system: shoulder, hand, foot, eye unit, shadow | presence crosses frame before face; weight changes cave objects |
| Laertes | orchard layers, soil knees, pruning hand, reduced height | names trees before touching son; land knowledge precedes embrace |
| Poseidon | no stable body requirement; pressure grammar only | horizon, water, rope and human balance deform before manifestation |

Black-silhouette review is mandatory for principals and named opposing/recognition roles. Doubling rules from P3 remain unchanged.
