## Gate method

The audit combines exact-text scanning with a full-season editorial read. Counts are regenerated by `editorial/odyssey_m1_v2/tools/audit_v2.py`; they are evidence, not a substitute for reading. The audit distinguishes intentional continuity—an object or phrase recurring because its custody matters—from reusable author scaffolding that could survive a character-name swap.

## Voice-swap challenge

The audit sampled all major pairings and asked whether role names could be exchanged without rewriting vocabulary, tactic, and action. They could not:

- Odysseus bargains through inventory, routes, names, and controlled omissions; his old need to claim authorship returns at the Cyclops shore.
- Penelope audits cloth, food, keys, witnesses, gifts, weapons, and finally private construction knowledge. She does not speak as a waiting oracle.
- Telemachus begins with broken syntax and borrowed authority; by EP30 he specifies witnesses, venue, and procedure, then cuts his own staff.
- Athena changes conditions and asks operational questions; she never supplies the human choice.
- Antinous seizes ownership bluntly; Eurymachus converts danger into a proposed deal; Amphinomus hesitates without acquiring a modern conscience.
- Eumaeus speaks through portions, gates, animals, weather, and work; aristocratic rhetoric cannot be substituted into his scenes.

Result: `PASS_CHARACTER_NAME_SWAP_RESISTANCE`.

## Hook and scene-function diversity

Opening pressures rotate among unlawful consumption, public humiliation, ritual error, contradictory testimony, forced departure, bodily vulnerability, forensic evidence, involuntary memory, command failure, hunger, closed-room weapon control, and civic retaliation. Ending hooks alternate between route threat, identity demand, object custody, concealed cost, forced interview, public test, first kill, armed reversal, private recognition, and unfinished civic work.

No two episode hooks are text-identical. The five-scene architecture is retained for format continuity, but scene functions are not interchangeable: each episode contains a different sequence of task, obstruction, reversal, cost, and forward pressure.
