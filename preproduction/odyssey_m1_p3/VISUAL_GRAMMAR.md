Status: `FROZEN_VISUAL_GRAMMAR`

## Image rule

An image earns recurrence only if its physical state, custody or meaning changes. Motifs are memory aids and evidence carriers, not decorative symbolism.

## Unit palette and texture

- S1 Ithaca: dry limestone, olive smoke, worn bronze, coarse wool; ownership changes, architecture does not.
- S2 ceremonial courts: pale cloth/polished wood with distinct redress signatures for Pylos, Sparta and Phaeacia.
- S3 deck: tar, salt, strained rope and changing sail state; no pristine reset between losses.
- S4 farm: earth, animal heat, repaired fiber, portions and tools; loyalty is tactile work.
- S5 shore/cave: reversible silhouette, wetness, vegetation and sound direction create distinct islands.
- U09 Underworld: black reflective floor, depth gauze, blood warmth; no ordinary sky direction.
- U10 orchard/field: specific numbered trees and worked soil; finale scale comes from lineage and divided hands.

## Color progression

Ithaca ochre/ash → Phaeacian chalk/blue → voyage rust/black/poison green → return mud/olive → battle bronze/red → aftermath undyed white joined to old purple. Grade supports photographed state and cannot disguise reused geometry by color alone.

## Composition progression

- EP01–04: Telemachus begins at frame edges and thresholds; authority often occupies center without him.
- EP05–15: Odysseus gains center through tactics, then loses companions at frame edges he cannot cover.
- EP16–25: foreground occlusion and long corridors make identity a restricted path.
- EP26–28: stable north and column lanes; no spectacle angle may destroy tactical comprehension.
- EP29–30: shared frames lengthen, then widen to include father, land and community.
