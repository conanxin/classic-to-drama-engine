# Material Language

Status: `FROZEN_P4_MATERIAL_LANGUAGE`

| Material | Truth test | Camera behavior | Continuity witness | Forbidden shorthand |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Olive timber | tool marks, growth twist, repaired checks | raking side light; macro only for makers' evidence | grip polish, cut, root, smoke | polished fantasy furniture |
| Ship timber | tar seams, salt bloom, compression and splinter | low moving line with actor balance | wet/dry edge, break and rope burn | pristine deck reset |
| Lime plaster | hand-floated, smoke and patch differences | broad soft source reveals repair plane | chips and blood cleanup residue | white-marble luxury |
| Limestone | weight, dust and irregular joints | stable geography, low specular | drag marks, hearth ash, tracked water | clean studio cyclorama |
| Bronze | localized hand polish and oxidation | small motivated highlight, never full gold wash | dent, blood/ash transfer | generic shining armor |
| Iron | limited to custody-critical blades/tools | darker value against skin/cloth | edge state and corrosion | high-fantasy black steel |
| Wool/linen | visible weave, repair and wet weight | hands and tension before beauty drape | stitch ID, tear, salt tide | fashion-editorial billow |
| Leather | compression, sweat darkening, repaired holes | medium/insert at use point | buckle hole and tie position | uniform brown armor |
| Rope | lay direction, tar, strain and fray | diagonal force line; sound priority | knot count, wet diameter | decorative nautical coil |
| Clay/oil | chip, soot, viscosity and residue | reflective detail only when custody changes | fill line, fracture, wash | decorative amphora wallpaper |
| Ash/salt/blood/water | exact accumulation and transfer | state evidence in shared composition | B/W ledgers and cleanup maps | atmosphere applied without cause |

Across sets, difference comes from use: Ithaca repairs and reuses; Phaeacia maintains and inventories; ship units strain and salt; Eumaeus' farm mends; shore/cave units abrade; Underworld absorbs ordinary color while preserving blood warmth.
