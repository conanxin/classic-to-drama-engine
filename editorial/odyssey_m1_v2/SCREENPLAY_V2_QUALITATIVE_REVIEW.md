# 《归途：奥德修斯》Screenplay V2 Stratified Qualitative Review

Status: `PASS_STRATIFIED_QUALITATIVE_REVIEW`

## Review standard

The required stratified sample—EP01, EP02, EP03, EP05, EP10, EP15, EP20, EP25, EP27, EP28, EP29, EP30—was read scene by scene after the full rewrite. Each scene was tested as screenplay rather than schema: playable objective, visible obstruction, irreversible state change, character-specific speech, editorial tempo, production feasibility, and source responsibility.

`PASS` below means the dramatic event exists on the page and can be staged. It does not mean the scene is protected from later director or actor refinement.

## Cross-sample findings and repairs

The read caught and repaired duplicate adjacent speaker cues, abstract summaries of growth/innocence/victory, a reversed question-and-answer in EP28, Penelope’s under-participation in EP30, and several places where an object was named but not used. Those lines were replaced with behavior: door bolts, knife custody, bow placement, witness lists, a stopped prompt, a shared boundary stone, and a new staff cut from orchard wood.

No sampled scene is merely an information handoff. No sampled scene requires an actor to “play a realization” without a target. No sampled episode relies on empty landscape to reach its runtime. The action-heavy EP27–28 scenes contain sufficient geography and inventory for director blocking; EP29–30 retain aftermath rather than pretending killing is closure.
