# Look Bible — A Worked World That Must Recognize Its Owner

Status: `FROZEN_P4_LOOK_BIBLE`

## Visual proposition

《归途：奥德修斯》 happens in a mythic Mediterranean that has been handled before the camera arrives. Salt blooms in timber joints; lime plaster carries repairs; bronze is polished only where hands repeatedly touch it; wool remembers rain and bodies. Home is a labor system, sea is a bodily pressure, disguise changes access, and recognition is evidence accumulated through people and worked objects.

The image must never default to white-marble fantasy, generic fantasy armor, permanent divine glow or blue-orange spectacle. Sets share construction logic across units. Redress, weather, work state, custody and light distinguish places without making each island a new franchise.

## Camera and design

Production design presents a usable route before the camera chooses emphasis. Doors, work surfaces, rope, keys, weapons and witnesses remain readable in wides. A close image is earned when an object changes the standard of proof. Moving masters follow tactics across real space; they do not float over exposition. P3 shot IDs, screen direction and S1 north remain immutable.

## Palette and contrast

- Ithaca: olive smoke, dry ochre, worn bronze, old purple, ash and undyed wool. Occupation adds wine/rust without repainting the house.
- Phaeacia: chalk, sea-glass blue and repaired cloth; hospitality is luminous but supervised.
- Voyage memory: tar black, rope umber, oxidized green and controlled blood rust; color drains as the crew diminishes.
- Return: mud, olive bark and low saturation; proof accents recover in scar rose, bow honey-brown and thread purple.
- Battle: bronze, soot and finite blood layers. Red is a state ledger, not a grade.
- Restoration: undyed white joins old purple; blacks lift slightly but damage remains.

Black level is story information. Occupation keeps corners legible but socially blocked; cave and Underworld lose horizon, not texture; recognition closes use narrow falloff around hands and shared faces; EP30 restores exterior depth without erasing ash.

## Texture and material system

Primary surfaces are timber, lime/plaster, stone, bronze, limited iron, rope, wool, linen, leather, olive wood, clay, ash, salt, blood, oil and water. Every hero surface has a use map: grip polish, repair stitch, smoke edge, wet line, blood transfer or tool mark. Ornament without custody or labor is excluded.

## Weather and sea

Weather arrives through costume weight, airborne salt, rope noise, unstable footing and exposure before landscape. Sea is heard before image. Horizon wides are withheld until balance, route or loss changes. Wet continuity uses W0 dry, W1 spray, W2 soaked, W3 drained-salt and W4 rewarmed; a later state never silently regresses.

## Interiors, night and fire

Interior depth comes from practical sources and blocked routes: hearth, oil lamp, doorway, high slit, reflected courtyard. Night keeps faces readable through motivated fire or sky bounce; moon-blue fill is not a default. Fire reveals work and risk—hearth tending, heated stake, sulfur cleanup—rather than providing generic amber atmosphere.

## Divine grammar

The altered condition precedes the god: crowd rhythm falls out of sync, reflected light lands incorrectly, cloth moves against the air, a shadow arrives one beat early, or sound loses its expected source. Athena has human disguises, an almost-recognized state and a restrained divine state; Poseidon removes balance and stable horizon. No divinity completes the decisive human choice.

## Violence and blood

Violence consumes arrows, exits, furniture, breath, trust and bodies. Each cut must preserve cause, contact, consequence and the incoming/outgoing state. Blood progresses B0 clean → B1 first impact → B2 first exchanges → B3 armory reversal → B4 full battle → B5 sulfur aftermath. Gore insert coverage is rejected when the same consequence is more legible in floor, cloth, grip or changed distance.

## Memory and recognition

Memory begins from a physical trigger and returns to a changed listener; it is never a neutral prestige flashback. Recognition escalates through name → story → scar → bow → axes → bed → father/land → community. Face alone is insufficient. Argos, scar, bow, bed and Laertes frames prioritize shared space, hands, eyelines and refusal over poster scale.

## Internal consistency gate

The style is approved only when a frame can answer: what is worked, who controls access, what state changed, which evidence is visible, and what remains physically possible. Any image that could be moved unchanged into unrelated generic fantasy is rejected.
