## Continuity and call rules

1. Every foreground ensemble performer receives `POOL-ID / FACE-ID / WARDROBE-ID / FIRST-SCENE / LAST-SCENE` and a left/right/360 photograph.
2. The S1 suitors carry stable table position, contest order, weapon access, injury, death and body-removal fields. A reset may not repopulate a dead foreground identity.
3. Sailor pools use ship-specific rope burns and shoulder tabs. The six Scylla victims are named positions, not interchangeable background.
4. Phaeacian brightness is production design and behavior—open lateral space and coordinated service—not only clean costumes.
5. Underworld reuse requires silhouette or deep-layer treatment; it may never create the impression that a living named identity died offscreen.
6. Crowd plates are captured by pool and state. Plate extension may multiply density but cannot create a new consequential witness, attacker or reconciler.
7. No call exceeds the practical safety, holding, costume and meal capacity recorded on the call sheet. Reduce the far layer before compressing supervision.
