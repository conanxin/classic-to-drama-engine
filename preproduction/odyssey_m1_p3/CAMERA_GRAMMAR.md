# Camera Grammar

Status: `FROZEN_CAMERA_GRAMMAR`

## Default behavior

The camera stays close enough to read hands, tasks, thresholds and evidence, and wide enough to preserve who can leave. It moves when a tactic crosses real space, not to decorate dialogue.

## Coverage architecture

- 831 planned shots / 150 scenes / 5.54 average.
- PERFORMANCE-CRITICAL: 14 pure performance scenes plus one technical hybrid; ratio 1.40–1.79.
- ACTION-CRITICAL: 30 scenes; ratio about 2.02 with isolated contact and reset plates.
- VFX-CRITICAL: 11 scenes; ratio 1.89–1.91 with practical, clean, reference and interaction coverage.
- STANDARD: 94 scenes; ratio about 1.52 without generic five-angle packages.

The ratios are verified in `SHOT_LIST_MASTER.json`; every scene currently reports PASS.

## Set-specific camera rules

### S1 Ithaca

- Use the frozen north orientation and position IDs. A new axis after a wall removal needs a geography re-establish.
- EP20–25 uses longer lenses/foreground doorframes as options close.
- EP26–28 action begins with a readable north; column, door, table and arrow changes motivate closer coverage.
- EP29 begins in damaged/cleaned space but moves to shared bedroom frames. The bed is not photographed as a reveal before Penelope’s instruction creates the test.

### S2 ceremonial hall

- Lateral shared frames compare hospitality and scrutiny.
- Redress identity is protected through portal shape, queen/work axis and floor/cloth lines rather than a new lens gimmick.

### S3/U11 ship and wet work

- Establish deck direction, mast, oar line and danger side once per condition change.
- Motion base, actors’ balance, rope and sail precede horizon spectacle.
- Wet inserts and VFX plates isolate principals from unsafe water/rig paths.

### S4 farm

- Low walls, gate and animal/work foreground create honest social distance.
- EP19 father/son recognition favors a continuous two-shot; transformation technical plates never replace disbelief performance.

### S5/U12 creature environments

- Scale uses foreground mismatch, controlled perspective, partial practical contact and reaction.
- Creature POV is permitted only when it changes tactical knowledge.
- No full digital hero reveal is required.

### U09 Underworld

- Camera height and blood access determine power; unequal interview rhythms prevent a row of identical dead-person reverses.
- Gauze layers and clean plates are planned before crowd plates.

### U10 finale

- Orchard rows and boundary stone establish worked land, not pastoral release.
- Field action remains limited and readable. The final widening includes both kin groups and the shared stone.

## Performance-scene protection

- EP19: father and son stay in shared frame through refused touch and evidence exchange; camera may separate only after belief carries cost.
- EP23: hand→scar→basin→stopped speech is one causal chain; avoid reaction ping-pong.
- EP29: Penelope and Odysseus remain together through false bed order; private construction proof earns the first isolated close images.

## Violence camera

- Cause, safe isolated contact, consequence and continuity replace multi-angle impact repetition.
- Handheld begins only when spatial control is physically lost and ends when a plan returns.
- Blood is registered through changed floor/cloth/body state, not gore inserts.
- Children, animals, water, height, fire and weapons are covered in controlled separate units.

## Divine camera

Photograph the altered condition first. Athena may be discovered inside an existing composition; Poseidon removes stable horizon and introduces water pressure; Zeus is physical response and sound. No generic orbit, speed ramp or glow announces divinity.
