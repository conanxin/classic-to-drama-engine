5. **Pre-call top-up:** each shoot day repeats only the day’s beats; no unrehearsed escalation is added for coverage.

## Camera and plates

- Each action scene uses one orientation master before inserts. SH004 in EP27-S01/S02/S04/S05 and EP28-S01/S02/S03 is the technical/contact plate anchor.
- Clean plates: all three aisles, N0/E0/S0/A0/G0, each table phase, body-free floor, body-state floor, shield wall and maximum-damage hall.
- VFX plates: safe arrow flight, spear extension where necessary, impact debris, Athena shield/eye-light. VFX cannot add a weapon, victim or route.
- Sound wild tracks: bow release, numbered arrow impacts, shield materials, each spear class, table breaks, breath under shield, crowd retreat and post-battle room tone.

## Blood and reset

- Shoot within each phase from widest/cleanest to tightest/wettest, but preserve story-order phase progression B0→B6.
- Every foreground body has a floor coordinate, posture, visible wound and last verified scene. Body removal is an on-camera/evidenced event or does not occur.
- Telemachus’ wound begins in the designated A28 beat; sleeve tear, skin blood, wrap and later EP29/30 state are one ledger.
- Breakaway tables and shields have numbered hero/stunt/rest states. A reset photograph must show doors, weapons, arrows, bodies, blood, furniture and camera-safe lanes.

## Safety command

Stunt coordinator owns contact; armorer/property master owns weapon release; SFX owns breakaway/blood; 1st AD owns closed set and emergency lane; medic owns return-to-work. A safety veto cannot be overridden by director or schedule. No metal projectile is fired toward a person. No live blade enters the stunt zone.
