## Executive finding

V1 is a reliable source-traceable outline rendered in screenplay markup, not yet a production draft. Its event order, five-beat architecture, source references, and recognition logic are sound. Its weakness is dramatic embodiment: almost every scene names the intended function, gives one balanced three-line exchange, then explains that the function has happened. Actors receive positions to argue, but few playable tasks; directors receive themes, but few objects, entrances, reversals, or spatial consequences; editors receive five equal blocks with little internal tempo.

The formal issue baseline is 420 countable instances:

- 330 repeated meta-direction/template phrases: 11 exact skeletons repeated in all 30 episodes;
- 30 duration gaps: average dialogue is 202 Chinese characters per episode, insufficient for an honest seven-minute performance;
- 30 dialogue-structure gaps: every episode is dominated by symmetrical three-line exchanges;
- 30 duplicated hook instances: every final action is restated as a bold hook instead of producing a new screen event.

These 420 instances are the V2 resolution ledger. Qualitative episode findings below guide the rewrite but are not inflated into a pseudo-precise issue count.

## Whole-season repetition findings

Exact phrases appearing 30 times each include “空间先于人物发出压力”, “人物不再停留在判断上”, “一个可见动作改变相互距离”, “新事实落地”, “选择被执行而不是宣布”, “短暂的静默把转折坐实”, “最后的动作没有释放压力”, “画面在答案出现前切断”, “没有立刻追问”, “先改变了场面方向”, and “都明白，刚才那一步不能撤回”. They describe an author’s intention rather than photographable behavior.

Across the season, “那就” appears 17 times, “为什么” 10, “终于” 9, “名字” 17, and “主人” 23. These words are not forbidden, but the frequency reveals a shared rhetorical engine: A states a proposition, B reframes it as a clever question, A closes the theme. V2 must vary interruption, evasion, silence with a target, failed requests, physical bargaining, misnaming, overheard speech, and asymmetrical power.

### EP01《没有父亲的家》

- **1 Opening hook:** occupation is stated, not enacted; open on a suitor using Odysseus’ cup while a servant removes the household seal. **2 Episode want:** Telemachus wants one controllable corner of his own house. **3 Conflict:** Antinous and Penelope both restrain him for different reasons.

- **8 Dialogue specificity:** Telemachus sounds fully adult too early. **9 Subtext:** most intentions are spoken. **10 Character voice:** Athena and Eurycleia share the same polished coaching register.
- **11 Exposition load:** “twenty years / father / suitors” is repeated rather than shown in household accounting. **12 Visual action:** use the cup, seal, loom thread, spear, and locked storeroom. **13 Scene redundancy:** scenes 2–3 repeat Athena’s activation.
- **14 Shootability:** excellent standing-set potential; keep one hall and one small sleeping alcove. **15 Source/invention integrity:** PASS if Telemachus still calls the assembly and accepts the voyage without Penelope deciding for him.

## Rewrite mandate

V2 must preserve all source/event bindings and the M1 locks while replacing every one of the 330 meta-direction occurrences with concrete behavior. Each episode must gain enough playable action, interruption, reaction, and relationship-specific dialogue to sustain approximately seven minutes without padding. Hooks must be screen events, not repeated summaries. The goal is not longer prose; it is more consequential time.
