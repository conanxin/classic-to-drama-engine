## Cut rule

Cut when information, custody, access, injury, power, weather or belief changes. Do not cut merely because another character begins speaking. A shared frame should hold when the dramatic fact is that two people must endure the same moment.

## Scene-level structure

Five scenes do not receive equal length or identical internal arcs. Within each scene:

1. enter on active pressure or task;
2. stay until the obstruction is legible;
3. compress only when a tactic repeats without new evidence;
4. hold the irreversible consequence;
5. leave on a new problem, custody or route—not a spoken summary.

## Dialogue editing

- Preserve interruptions, unfinished thoughts and actor timing.
- Do not manufacture intelligence by removing hesitations from early Telemachus.
- Odysseus’ silence must reveal what he controls or fears losing; avoid reaction inserts that turn him into a commentator.
- Penelope’s withheld acceptance is active evaluation. Hold her work/object/eyeline with the claimant rather than isolating a “mysterious” face.
- Crowd reactions are used only when public legitimacy changes.

## Action editing

- EP10: establish cave mouth/giant routine before blinding; contact plates stay brief; ram escape returns to breath and touch.
- EP14: six grabs are six named positions, not one repeated creature shot; empty oar locks become the consequence beat.
- EP26–28: cut by the 44 action-beat authority. Arrow count, A0 door state, table phase, Telemachus wound and bow custody must survive every compression.
- Do not intercut divine VFX before human blocking has completed the choice that permits it.

## Memory and time

- A physical/sound trigger crosses into voyage memory; Phaeacian listener pressure returns before the memory acquits Odysseus.
- EP12’s year uses repeated doorway/task/costume wear, not a generic seasons montage.
- Underworld interviews vary entry, blood access, camera height, duration and exit; no equal five-person rhythm.

## Sound bridges

Sea before image, loom into oar rhythm, bow note into axes rings, basin rim into scar silence, bed wood into orchard bark, lowered spears into staff saw. Sound may bridge a visual transition only when the object/action relationship is established.

## Runtime lock

The 6:50–7:15 V2 estimates remain authoritative. Editorial economy removes redundant coverage, not playable tasks, evidence or consequence. No episode is lengthened with empty landscape or shortened by deleting protected recognition.
