## Battle consumables

- Arrow continuity: `12` physical story arrows for the contest state, with safe duplicates and VFX shafts kept outside story count. The passed test arrow is recovered before Antinous; the battle begins with an auditable eleven.
- Spears/shields: armory and hero loads are labeled by entrance path and scene phase. Stunt substitutions retain silhouette and patina.
- Blood: character-specific tones and locations; hall floor, clothing, cup, sulfur cleanup, and EP29 residue each have photographic continuity.
- Fire: shielded practical fire for hearth, pyre, and sulfur; lighting flicker cannot imply a flame larger than the safety setup.

## Asset governance

Every hero asset receives: identity photograph, dimensions, material, hero/stunt count, custodian, first scene, last scene, wear state, reset notes, sound sample, and disposal/archival status. Recognition assets are checked at call and wrap by script supervisor and property master together.
