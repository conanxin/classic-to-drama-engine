## Execution grammar

1. **Practical contact first:** actor, object, spray, rope, breakaway, prosthetic or shadow supplies the contact and timing.
2. **Limited comp second:** extend scale, horizon, depth, texture or disappearance. A comp cannot invent the decisive human action.
3. **Sound before reveal:** Poseidon, Cyclops, Scylla, Underworld and the petrifying ship begin as pressure and witness reaction.
4. **Clean plate is mandatory:** every matrix row lists its plate package; “fix it later” without a camera/lighting reference is not a plan.
5. **Eye-line and physical reference:** a measured target stays in the take. Creature size cannot drift between wide and close work.
6. **Editorial escape:** each effect has a practical reaction/object version that keeps story legible if the enhancement is reduced.
7. **Full-CG hero creature required:** `false`.
