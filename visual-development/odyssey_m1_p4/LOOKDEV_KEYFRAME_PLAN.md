# Lookdev Keyframe Plan

Status: `FROZEN_48_HERO_FRAME_SELECTION`

P4 selects 48 high-fidelity hero frames. The selection exceeds the 36-frame floor because character identity, the five standing sets, all eight creature/VFX systems, the recognition chain and the S1 battle progression require different review questions. These images are not a substitute for the 711 technical frames.

## Review families

- `HF01–HF08` — home/absence, Telemachus departure, Ogygia/raft, storm, Nausicaa and Phaeacian scrutiny.
- `HF09–HF16` — Cyclops practical scale/eye contact, fleet loss, Circe, Underworld, Sirens, Scylla and wreck.
- `HF17–HF24` — Ithaca return, Athena disguise, Eumaeus, father/son proof, Argos, weapon removal and scar.
- `HF25–HF34` — bow custody, twelve axes, first blood, arrow ledger, armory reversal, full battle, aftermath.
- `HF35–HF40` — bed test, husband/wife recognition, Laertes/land, boundary stone, weapons lowering, thread/staff closure.
- `HF41–HF48` — production anchors for Phaeacian hall, ship deck, farm, shore/cave, Underworld, S1 six-state identity and hero-prop evidence.

Recognition frames (Argos, scar, bow, bed, Laertes/land) use shared frames, hands, distance and eyelines. They exclude poster posing, isolated magic light and premature triumphant centers.

Every asset is created only after its manifest row freezes character/costume/HMU/wet/blood/set/prop/lighting bindings. ImageGen output without those bindings is rejected, even if attractive.
