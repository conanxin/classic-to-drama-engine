## Scar governance

- The boar scar is mapped on a body template with length, orientation, depth, color and edge photographs. It never changes side.
- Units: `SCAR-HERO` for EP23/29/30 close work, `SCAR-STUNT` for action, `SCAR-WET` for basin/wash and one protected reference mold.
- EP23 thumb-to-scar recognition must be practical. Digital cleanup may remove appliance edges but cannot invent contact.
- The scar proves bodily history to Eurycleia and supports later identity; it does not replace Penelope’s private bed verification.

## Blood, wet and prosthetic protocol

1. Blood uses character-coded formulas and maps: OD, TE, ANT, SUITOR and ENV. Costume, skin, prop and floor layers share the same action beat and timestamp.
2. Telemachus’ arm wound begins at its exact A28 beat; no pre-wound plate contains blood or a torn sleeve.
3. Cyclops eye blood, hall blood and Underworld blood are separate colors/viscosities; no audience-facing asset crosses departments.
4. Wet continuity records hair saturation, face droplets, garment weight, sand adhesion and drying minute. Dry dialogue plates precede wet escalation.
5. Circe transformations use partial prosthetics and matched hand/face states. No performer remains in an unsafe respiratory or restrictive appliance beyond the approved interval.
6. All prosthetic removals include skin checks and turnaround. Water, smoke, blood and adhesive combinations receive patch testing.

## Daily evidence

The HMU key records slate, scene, story state, left/right/front/back, calibrated card, appliance lot, blood map, wet level, application time, removal time and skin note. Script supervisor signs recognition and action transitions; stunt/VFX signs contact appliances.
