Status: `IN_PROGRESS`

Each block is read after its five-episode text exists. A PASS closes continuity, character progression, props, injuries, knowledge, location logic, hook diversity, and production load for that block; it does not replace the final whole-season review.

## EP01–EP05

- **Continuity:** EP01's concealed voyage becomes EP02's departure, EP03's witness search, EP04's live-prisoner evidence, and EP05's first sight of the prisoner himself. The chronology does not require Telemachus and Odysseus to know of each other's present actions.
- **Character arc:** Telemachus progresses through chair → staff → reins → contested testimony. He is observant by EP04 but still asks blunt questions, mishandles ceremony, and depends on older hosts; he has not acquired his father's polished authority. Odysseus enters as a man whose first controlled identity decision is refusing immortality, not delivering a clever slogan.
- **Prop continuity:** dolphin cup stays in Ithaca; the shortened sword is replaced by Telemachus's own knife before travel; the assembly staff breaks and remains behind; Nestor's black ship nail travels to Sparta; Book 4's sea-lion hide is witness evidence, not a recognition object. No prop teleports.
- **Injury continuity:** Telemachus's rope-cut palm in EP02 can close during EP03–04 and is not used as a later identity proof. Odysseus's raft-building forearm cut and salt abrasions carry into EP06.
- **Knowledge state:** Penelope knows Telemachus asserted public standing but does not know his departure at EP02 launch. Telemachus learns “alive with Calypso” in EP04; he does not know an escape has been ordered. Suitors know his return route but not what he learned. Odysseus knows nothing about Ithaca's current household.
- **Location continuity:** Ithaca hall/courtyard and harbor are standing units; Pylos shore and the Odyssey shore/cave environment share a coastal construction family but retain different dressing; Sparta is a redress of the second hall unit. The waterway ambush and raft storm use different scale grammars.
- **Hook diversity:** secret plan → intercepted departure → new witness → trap at home → vulnerable landfall. No two adjacent hooks use the same information mechanism.
- **Production load:** EP01 low/medium, EP02 medium, EP03 medium, EP04 medium, EP05 high. The block ramps to one high water episode rather than sustaining costly spectacle.

## EP06–EP10

- **Continuity:** EP05's salt wounds and naked landfall create EP06's rope boundary and borrowed garment; the garment becomes EP07 forensic evidence. EP07's blank ship plate becomes EP08's name demand. The spoken name opens EP09's testimony and is catastrophically repeated in EP10.
- **Character arc:** Odysseus first controls his body before瑙西卡, then controls omission before阿瑞忒, loses bodily control to memory/song, and finally demonstrates the old flaw: a successful concealed identity is exposed for glory. This is a visible exposed-cleverness baseline, not a flat succession of clever wins.
- **Prop continuity:** 卡吕普索's blue repair thread remains inside the white 菲埃克斯 garment; the garment is evidence, not a permanent costume. The empty ship plate is named and later carved. The 玛戎 wine enters with the cave party and is consumed in the escape. “No one” is a temporary operational name, explicitly destroyed by the true-name shout.
- **Injury continuity:** Salt abrasions remain in EP06, are washed/oiled rather than magically erased, and the weak right leg affects EP08's throw. Torn fingers in EP08 provide a physical cut into the war recollection. Cave losses are counted through shrinking survivor groups.
- **Knowledge state:** 菲埃克斯 rulers know the full name only at EP08; they do not possess the voyage history before his account. The audience sees that the account is self-accusing rather than objective omniscience. Ithaca and Telemachus remain off this five-episode clock.
- **Location continuity:** shore/cave adaptable environment handles EP06 and EP09–10 with distinct dressing; 菲埃克斯 hall is a standing set; its courtyard carries athletics. Creature scale never requires a full digital Cyclops in a wide shot.
- **Hook diversity:** incriminating garment → withheld name → name revealed → sealed cave → curse attached to name. The common recognition theme advances through different physical carriers.
- **Production load:** EP06 medium, EP07 low/medium, EP08 medium, EP09 high, EP10 high. EP09–10 are scheduled as one cave block and share creature, animal, fire, and blood units.

## EP11–EP15

- **Continuity:** EP10's named sea curse drives EP11's wind failure and fleet destruction. The one surviving flagship and reduced crew enter EP12; a full year is marked on the same remaining unit. EP13 supplies the cattle prohibition invoked in EP15. EP14's six empty oar stations materially reduce the crew before the final loss.
- **Character arc:** Odysseus withholds the wind bag's truth, withholds the six-death forecast, and tries to own the cattle prohibition through command. Each concealment has a different tactical rationale but compounds the same legitimacy failure. The 菲埃克斯 frame refuses a heroic acquittal: he is believed, transported, and still judged.
- **Prop continuity:** wind bag is visibly emptied and torn; Circe's repaired belt signals a year but is not carried as recognition proof; six bone counters move from briefing to concealment to disposal; the sun-cattle badge is rejected into the sea. The carved 伊萨卡 ship plate closes the story frame and passes to the transport authority.
- **Injury continuity:** EP10 cave survivors begin as the sole remaining fleet population; EP11 reduces twelve ships to one; EP14 takes six named rowers; EP15 kills every remaining companion. No later companion can reappear. Odysseus's recurrent water injuries accumulate rather than reset.
- **Knowledge state:** crew hears Tiresias's prohibition in EP13 through欧律洛科斯, so EP15's breach is informed. Crew does not learn the six-person price until the strait entrance. 菲埃克斯 audience now knows the account but not objective facts outside his testimony; their reservations remain explicit.
- **Location continuity:** the flagship deck is one standing unit across all five; Circe and underworld use separate dressings of the adaptable environment; EP14–15 water effects batch with EP11. High-cost elements are grouped rather than recreated per episode.
- **Hook diversity:** fleet reduced → road to dead → father/son question → six-death accountability → morally unresolved transport. Spectacle hooks alternate with ethical hooks.
- **Production load:** EP11 high, EP12 medium, EP13 medium/high, EP14 high, EP15 high. A single water/ship block covers EP11, EP14, EP15; creature/VFX grammar stays partial and sound-led.

## EP16–EP20

- **Character arc:** Odysseus lies even before confirming home, then tests loyalty, then permits his son to doubt. Telemachus applies hospitality learned at Pylos, makes his own coastal decision, verifies rather than worships the returned father, and keeps the first dangerous secret from Penelope. Neither speaks with fully settled adult authority.

## EP21–EP25

- **Continuity:** the EP20 stool assault becomes EP21's second throw and broken loom frame; the cleared hall permits EP22 weapon removal. Garment testimony triggers washing; washing triggers scar recognition; the scar secret enables loyal-herdsmen verification. Penelope's bow decision becomes a physically calibrated twelve-axe contest.
- **Character arc:** Odysseus controls a fight, warns a potentially decent enemy, endures spouse scrutiny, and delegates door control. Penelope converts courtship into a named debt ledger, rejects both perfect testimony and dream certainty, then constructs a public repeatable test. Telemachus chooses not to string the bow and accepts operational authority without stealing recognition.
- **Prop continuity:** weapons move with exact inventory and remain in the locked store; neither suitors nor heroes may fetch them without the later armory route. Purple cloth/gold dog clasp authenticate a meeting but not identity. Scar authenticates only to Eurycleia/herdsmen. Bow, bent key, twelve axes, door keys, and arrow line all have visible custody.

- **Hook diversity:** emptied weapons → physical scar → public test → closed room → weapon in beggar's hand. Recognition objects carry escalating access, not repeated surprise faces.
- **Production load:** EP21 medium, EP22 low/medium, EP23 low, EP24 low/medium, EP25 medium. Crowd days and hall coverage can be shot as a continuous block; prop continuity is more demanding than VFX.

## EP26–EP30

- **Continuity:** EP25 door keys and bow custody open EP26; the test arrow is recovered before Antinous, leaving an auditable eleven-arrow inventory. EP27 reduces the quiver to three, then zero in EP28. The forgotten armory lock causes the weapon reversal and is closed by the herdsmen. Hall blood is cleaned before marital recognition but remains in grout as consequence.
- **Character arc:** Odysseus ends anonymity by action, refuses a property settlement, accepts Telemachus's mistake without turning the battle into blame, then yields final verification to Penelope. Telemachus stands beside, kills and names the cost, admits the armory error, argues with his mother, learns to step back, stops his spear, and begins making his own assembly staff. Penelope's private bed test overrules public victory.
- **Prop continuity:** exact bow/arrow/axe path is locked; the double-eared cup is recovered and washed, not magically pristine. Armory gear follows the dark route. Olive bed is immovable and rooted. Purple year-thread changes function after return. Three generations' spears are visibly distinct; Telemachus ends by making a new civic staff rather than inheriting the broken one.
- **Injury continuity:** Telemachus receives an upper-arm cut in EP28, wraps it, and can still throw but not without cost. Odysseus carries accumulated bruising into EP29. Laertes is old but throws once from prepared footing; he does not become a sustained action hero.
- **Knowledge state:** Penelope learns only after battle and does not accept secondhand proof. Public identity, scar identity, bow identity and bed identity remain separate. Laertes requires land/tree knowledge beyond scar. Community learns responsibility through the field confrontation and promised assembly, not divine memory erasure.
- **Location continuity:** EP26–28 share one fully mapped hall/armory block. EP29 reuses hall and bedroom. EP30 reuses underworld then moves to one orchard/field unit. Battle scale decreases from interior explosion to limited civic clash.
- **Hook diversity:** first kill → armed reversal → spouse question → civic retaliation → closure. EP30 ends on labor (a new staff being cut), not a summary slogan.
- **Production load:** EP26 medium/high, EP27 high, EP28 high, EP29 low/medium, EP30 high. Shoot EP26–28 sequentially by damage state; shoot clean EP29 coverage before blood stages; orchard and field share one exterior company move.
