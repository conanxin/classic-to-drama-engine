# Participant Instructions

You are testing how easy a story is to follow, not your knowledge of *The Odyssey* and not your intelligence.

1. Use an anonymous code; do not enter your name or email.
2. Read the three assigned parts in order. One or two use a screenplay layout; the others use the Graphic reading layout.
3. Use any character, relationship or source help you naturally notice. There is no penalty for opening help.
4. After each part, return to the test page and answer from memory. Do not reopen the episode while answering.
5. If you become lost, say or write where it happened. That is useful product evidence.
6. You may stop at any time. Nothing uploads automatically.
7. At the end, choose **Export Reader Test Result** and give the downloaded JSON file to the person running the study only if you wish to share it.
