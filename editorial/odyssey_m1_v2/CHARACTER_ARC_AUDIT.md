Her evidence chain must be visible: **garment detail → manner of lying → scar report (not sufficient) → bow capability/public claim → bed construction/private fact**.

Telemachus V2 correction: early language must be visibly unsteady. ## Relationship and recognition checkpoints

- **EP01–04:** Telemachus learns that identity is testimony plus action, preparing him to doubt a magically transformed father.
- **EP05–15:** Odysseus’ narrated failures establish why concealment in Ithaca is growth rather than mere plot device.
- **EP16–19:** place, disguise, loyalty, and son become successive recognition tests.
- **EP20–25:** Argos, household servants, garment, scar, dream, bow, and axes accumulate but remain partial.
- **EP26–28:** public capability and political identity are established through violence; marital identity remains open.
- **EP29:** the rooted bed resolves private identity.
- **EP30:** fruit trees and land resolve lineage; lowering weapons resolves community membership conditionally.
