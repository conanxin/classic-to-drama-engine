Each MUST shot has at least one frame. Moving, stunt or medium-VFX shots normally receive START/END frames. The frame set preserves practical base, actor path, contact/interaction, clean/reference needs, continuity state and intended composite.

### SHOULD STORYBOARD

Performance recognition, LOW VFX, stunt, creature or high-complexity scenes outside the mandated episode set receive representative GEOGRAPHY, REVERSAL, CONSEQUENCE or HOOK frames. These are planning frames, not complete animatics.

### NO STORYBOARD REQUIRED

Low-complexity performance/task scenes with legible director blocking and shot lists do not receive placeholder images. This protects rehearsal time and avoids turning every dialogue scene into diagram-driven performance.

## Frame schema

Every planned frame records:

- `frame_id` and parent `shot_id`;
- composition and characters;
- foreground, midground and background;
- camera height and movement phase;
- lighting premise;
- practical element;
- VFX layer;
- continuity state.
