# Character Introduction Budget

status: `FROZEN_FOR_P7C_TEST`

## Budget

A first viewport may introduce at most **three individual names plus one clearly labelled group**. A hard maximum of four independent names applies before the first dramatic action. A group counts as half an introduction only when the page does not simultaneously split it into several named members.

This is an editorial product rule, not a cognitive-science norm. It exists so P7C can compare like with like.

## Progressive levels

- **Level 1 — Glance:** symbol or portrait, name, one short role. Always available; no biography.
- **Level 2 — Context:** current scene relation, current status and faction. Opened by hover/focus preview or tap/click disclosure.
- **Level 3 — Detail:** recognition anchors, aliases, previous verified action and identity rules. Closed by default in the episode cast rail.

## EP19 budget

Four identities are permissible because the problem is identity layering rather than crowd size. Level 1 labels 奥德修斯 as the reader-known person while scene context preserves the old-man disguise and Telemachus's uncertainty.

## EP27 budget

Priority first four: 奥德修斯, 忒勒马科斯, 众求婚者, 欧律马科斯. 安菲诺摩斯、欧迈俄斯、菲洛提俄斯 and 墨兰提俄斯 enter through scene context. The group label remains visible until individual responsibility changes.

## Failure rules

A viewport is over budget when it presents five or more new independent names at equal weight, opens Level 3 by default, or requires a reader to remember a relationship before any action demonstrates it. The remedy is sequencing and disclosure, never deleting story responsibility.
