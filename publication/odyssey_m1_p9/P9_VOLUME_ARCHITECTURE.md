# P9 Volume Architecture

status: `FROZEN_P9_VOLUME_ARCHITECTURE`

The publication is divided by the five frozen P7B story movements, not by an equal episode quota. Every volume begins with a dramatic promise, completes a distinct geography/identity movement, and ends on a forward-driving change.

| Volume | Chapters | Title | Dramatic unit | Closing turn |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| V01 | EP01–EP04 | 没有父亲的家 | 伊萨卡失序与出航 | 忒勒马科斯 leaves home carrying other people's testimony |
| V02 | EP05–EP08 | 重新说出名字 | 脱离停滞与重获身份 | 奥德修斯 publicly names himself and opens the embedded account |
| V03 | EP09–EP15 | 海上的名字与代价 | 漂流自述 | the last ship closes the wanderings and points home |
| V04 | EP16–EP24 | 乞丐回到门槛 | 返乡、伪装与忠诚测试 | every loyalty test converges on the bow contest |
| V05 | EP25–EP30 | 弓、婚床与归位 | 清算、识别与共同体 | public peace completes the private recognitions |

The 4/4/7/9/6 chapter distribution is intentionally uneven. V03 needs room for the nested voyage; V04 is the long compression of return and testing. No episode or scene is moved across its formal arc.
