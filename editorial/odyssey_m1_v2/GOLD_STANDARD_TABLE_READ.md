# 《归途：奥德修斯》EP01–EP03 Gold Standard Table-read

Status: `GOLD_STANDARD_PASS`

Review scope: `scripts/odyssey_m1_v2/episodes/EP01.md` through `EP03.md`

Method: the three episodes were read straight through once from each of five production viewpoints. Findings below describe the first V2 pass; every blocking finding was corrected in the screenplay files before this record was frozen. This is an internal editorial simulation and used no external model or paid service.

## Actor read

- **EP01:** Telemachus can play embarrassment through the oversized sword and the occupied chair instead of declaring adolescence. Penelope has executable household tactics: she seizes the wine inventory, orders three public account copies, and protects her political position without becoming passive exposition. The final unexplained harbor lamp gave Telemachus nothing playable and was removed; his choice now lands in exchanging his father's sword for his own knife and rotating the route tablet.
- **EP02:** the first pass made the assembly debate too efficient and gave the elders too little personal stake. Aegyptius now names his missing son through the absence of a grave, Antinous defends the suitors with concrete gifts, and the old sailor tests Telemachus's unworked hands. Telemachus still sounds like a young man trying to hold a line, not an accomplished king.
- **EP03:** Nestor's testimony is carried by ship nails, coals, and a scalding cup. Telemachus's wrong libation and bad first turn of the reins give the actor a visible learning curve. The transformation scene now states in the episode lock that Athena's departure, not merely the later road choice, is the source-preserved irreversible turn.

Result: `PASS_PLAYABLE_INTENT_AND_BEHAVIOR`

## Director read

- Every scene has an entrance, task, obstruction, object transaction, or spatial reconfiguration.
- EP01 uses the same hall from three camera axes: occupied central table, guest-law threshold, and stripped side inventory. No new set is created for the accounting beat.
- EP02 re-dresses the Ithaca courtyard as assembly ground and contains open-water photography to a short practical near-shore departure. The ambush seed is a hand-sized pottery insert, not a second naval unit.
- EP03's divine transformation uses lamp pressure, wing sound, an eagle shadow, and an empty mark; it does not require an on-camera digital morph. The chariot can be photographed with a process trailer, inserts, and one wide second-unit pass.

Result: `PASS_DIRECTABLE_SCREEN_GRAMMAR`

## Editor read

- EP01 accelerates occupation → private challenge → public declaration → secret departure decision; scene 3 no longer repeats scene 2 because it establishes the forty-day resource clock.
- EP02's debate has three distinct turns: standing to speak, public claim, and the loom counter-accusation. The added personal testimony prevents the assembly from reading as a three-line argument.
- EP03 changes rhythm deliberately: communal ritual, forensic testimony, warning parable, divine rupture, practical road competence.
- Each ending opens the next risk rather than restating the episode lesson.

Result: `PASS_ESCALATION_AND_CUT_POINTS`

## Producer read

- The Gold Standard uses four reusable production families: Ithaca hall/courtyard, harbor/near-shore, Pylos shore, and a redressed court plus road unit.
- EP01 has no VFX. EP02 requires one practical boat and no offshore stunt. EP03's sacrificed animal is already prepared food and inserts; no live sacrificial action is staged. The initial animal tag was removed.
- Crowd loads are concentrated into one setup per episode and can be shot in blocks. Hero objects are reusable and plot-bearing: dolphin cup, assembly staff, white thread, black ship nail.
- No direction requires unsafe improvisation, an unbounded crowd, or an unmotivated weather day.

Result: `PASS_PRODUCTION_FEASIBILITY`

## First-time viewer read

- Within twenty seconds EP01 shows whose house this is and who currently controls it.
- EP02 makes the public failure legible even without prior knowledge: the crowd hears Telemachus but refuses to touch his staff; the white thread redirects blame to Penelope.
- EP03 answers one question (“Nestor does not know”) while creating a sharper one (“what did Menelaus see?”). Athena's exit does not solve Telemachus's task.
- The three hooks vary: concealed decision, enemy interception, acquired competence/new witness.

Result: `PASS_CLARITY_AND_FORWARD_PULL`

## Gold-standard locks carried into EP04–EP30

1. Five scenes remain functional units, not five summaries.
2. Every scene changes ownership, access, knowledge, risk, route, alliance, or bodily position through visible action.
3. Dialogue names an immediate object, task, debt, danger, or relationship before it names a theme.
4. A god may change conditions, expose a route, or withdraw protection; the human still performs the consequential choice.
5. Recognition objects receive a memorable first behavior or sound before later proof.
6. Medium-cost grammar is the default: practical foreground, partial construction, sound, shadow, restricted viewpoint, and editorial implication.
7. An episode cannot pass merely because it contains five headings, source IDs, or a final bold hook.
