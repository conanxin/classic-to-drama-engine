# Casting Breakdown

Status: `PASS_CASTING_BREAKDOWN`

Authority: 30 V2 episodes, 150 scenes, four principals and 65 normalized nonprincipal credited identities. The identity-by-identity class, scene count and allowed doubling are frozen in `CASTING_PRIORITY_MATRIX.md`.

## Principal briefs

### Odysseus

- **Screen age:** 38–48; a face that can plausibly carry ten years of salt, command and hunger without makeup doing all the work.
- **Presence:** dangerous competence that can be deliberately reduced. He must make concealment feel like labor, not passive charisma.
- **Voice:** low-to-mid register, variable pace; the boast accelerates while a lie becomes simpler and quieter. No default “hero speech.”
- **Stillness:** can receive an insult without signaling the coming revenge; breath and hand pressure do more than a glare.
- **Movement:** sailor balance, carpenter hands, economical fighting, credible bow mechanics and an altered beggar gait that never becomes caricature.
- **Authority / vulnerability:** command must coexist with shame over the six Scylla losses, the wind bag and the exposed name.
- **Comic capacity:** dry tactical humor and social improvisation; never a contemporary quip machine.
- **Physical demand:** raft and wet stage, shore crawl, forced-scale Cyclops contact, farm labor, bow draw, three-aisle hall battle.
- **Stunt / water:** swim confidence test; SD-OD-WATER and SD-OD-FIGHT integration; principal keeps all choice, name, bow-hand and recognition beats.
- **Availability risk:** highest. Hold for all S3/S5/U11 work, S4 father-son block, S1 disguise/battle/recognition and U10 finale; no conflicting facial-hair job across the shoot.

### Penelope

- **Screen age:** 36–46; must read as Telemachus’ mother and the active manager of a besieged estate.
- **Presence:** can change a room by moving a key, gift, thread or chair rather than raising volume.
- **Voice:** exact nouns and short operational questions; warmth appears through practical care, anger through procedure.
- **Stillness:** investigative rather than statuesque. Eye-line stays with the object or witness being tested.
- **Movement:** loom, accounts, stairs, bow-box custody and bed-threshold geometry must feel habitual.
- **Authority / vulnerability:** household command never cancels exhaustion, desire, embarrassment or the fear of authenticating the wrong man.
- **Comic capacity:** restrained knowledge of household absurdity; no sardonic modern detachment.
- **Physical demand:** low stunt, high hand-continuity and long-take performance demand; repeated S1 stair and threshold routes.
- **Stunt / water:** none required; stand-in is lighting only. Her hands may not be doubled for loom, key, bow, cup or bed-test evidence.
- **Availability risk:** protect EP22–25 and EP29 as continuous performance blocks even if S1 clean material is shot earlier.

### Telemachus

- **Screen age:** 18–23; young enough that EP01 public failure is credible, old enough to grow into armed civic responsibility.
- **Presence:** initially occupies edges and borrows posture; later holds a doorway and a shared frame without imitating Odysseus.
- **Voice:** early clauses restart; travel questions become narrower; battle calls are brief and practical; final civic speech is earned, not sonorous.
- **Stillness:** improves across blocks. EP01 nervous hand-work becomes EP19 secret-keeping and EP30 watchfulness.
- **Movement:** horse, boat, staff, key, armory route, shield and spear. He must make a missed latch and an arm wound readable.
- **Authority / vulnerability:** still wants recognition from the father he must also judge.
- **Comic capacity:** youthful defensiveness and occasional awkward candor, never precocious wit.
- **Physical demand:** travel, rowing, short falls, shield/spear battle, wound continuity.
- **Stunt / water:** SD-TE-ACTION for horse edge, boat edge and battle contacts; principal performs speeches, latch, wound discovery and father-son tactics.
- **Availability risk:** second-highest; production order crosses boy/traveler/returning heir/battle/civic states, so voice and body-state rehearsal is mandatory.

### Athena

- **Screen age:** 28–45 in divine state; disguises may shift apparent age while keeping one intelligence.
- **Presence:** notices the operational weakness before the room does. Never plays generic benevolent mystery.
- **Voice:** rhythm changes by social mask—Mentes commercial, Mentor civic, girl brisk, boy competitive, shepherd local—but consonant precision remains.
- **Stillness:** environmental pauses organize around her; she does not float or declaim.
- **Movement:** each disguise has a different center of gravity; the pin, eye-light and a repeated half-turn bind them.
- **Authority / vulnerability:** authority is absolute over conditions but limited by the rule that humans must choose. Vulnerability is interest in whether they will.
- **Comic capacity:** tactical amusement, never mockery of mortal pain.
- **Physical demand:** matched-position transformations, horse/harbor movement, one battle entrance, field truce.
- **Stunt / water:** BD-AT-MATCH only for exact transformation position; principal owns eyes, voice, intervention timing and the EP28/30 limits.
- **Availability risk:** costume/HMU changes create more schedule risk than scene count. Batch by disguise only after performance transitions are rehearsed.

## Selection process

1. **Recorded scene pair:** public and private beat for each principal; no montage reels as final evidence.
2. **Chemistry pairs:** Odysseus–Penelope use EP29 bed instruction; Odysseus–Telemachus use EP19 verification; Penelope–Telemachus use EP01 household command; Athena tests separately with both men.
3. **Object work:** bow trainer, loom/keys, staff/armory, disguise movement. An actor cannot pass on dialogue alone.
4. **Physical screen:** water confidence, fight movement, horse/boat comfort and prosthetic tolerance as applicable; a safety limit changes planning, not casting truth.
5. **Camera screen:** shared-frame performance on 40–65 mm and one controlled close-up. EP19/23/29 are not auditioned as alternating singles.
6. **Availability hold:** target schedule plus two weather/health buffers; principal state changes are contractual continuity requirements.

## Supporting strategy

- `MUST UNIQUE` identities protect family, recognition, command, betrayal, testimony and consequence.
- `CAN DOUBLE` applies only to the six tested non-overlap packages in the matrix. It is an option, not a savings assumption until hair, voice, gait and release separation pass camera review.
- Antinous’ EP30 dead identity is the same actor and is not a replacement booking.
- Polyphemus is a unique creature performance supported by a scale/stunt double; Poseidon is a unique voice/presence and cannot be reused as Zeus.
- Foreground suitors remain individually ledgered EP20–28. No visible suitor doubles as bereaved kin in EP30.
- The 65-identity count is a story identity count. Supplemental stunt/body doubles are production contracts and do not inflate it.
