# P10 Manual Submission Checklist

P10B prepares packages but performs no store submission. For each platform, the human publisher must:

1. Create or sign in to the official account.
2. Complete any identity verification and accept the current agreement.
3. Recheck the dated official requirements snapshot.
4. Decide platform identifier versus ISBN; current ISBN is `NOT_ASSIGNED`.
5. Review rights declarations and territories.
6. Decide price, currency, DRM and sales dates.
7. Complete tax and bank fields requested by the platform.
8. Upload only canonical assets named in the package manifest.
9. Run the platform preview on representative devices and resolve warnings.
10. Record the uploaded file digest and platform receipt.
11. Submit only after a human final review.

P10B does not request or store credentials, tax IDs, banking information or payment details.
