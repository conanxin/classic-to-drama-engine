# 《归途：奥德修斯》Format and Pacing Lock V2

Status: `LOCKED_30_X_APPROX_7_MINUTES`

## Master format

- Episodes: exactly 30.
- Target runtime: approximately 7 minutes each.
- Acceptable editorial range: 6:30–7:30; season average target 7:00.
- Primary scene count: five playable scenes per episode, retaining the 150-scene architecture. A scene may contain multiple beats, but a heading change is not used merely to inflate count.
- Language: contemporary performable Chinese in M1 mythic setting.
- Source: locked Books 1–24 event corpus; Greek semantic content is not required.
- Delivery level: production draft ready for director breakdown, not final shooting script.

No earlier duration target supersedes this lock. V1’s authoritative statement “approximately seven minutes per episode” remains master even though its actual written material is short.

## V1 duration discrepancy

V1 contains 24,742 Chinese characters in total, of which only 6,055 are spoken dialogue under a strict cue extraction. The remaining material includes metadata, repeated functional prose, source bindings, and summaries rather than timed action. Average spoken dialogue is 202 Chinese characters per episode.

At an acted short-drama rate of 210–240 Chinese characters per minute, V1 supplies roughly 0.84–0.96 minutes of speech per episode. Five summarized beats add an estimated 1.9–2.6 minutes of screen action if staged generously. Therefore most V1 episodes honestly estimate at 2.8–3.6 minutes, not seven. EP25–EP28 are shortest despite carrying the most complex action.

## V2 timing method

Runtime is estimated scene by scene, not from total character count alone:

1. **Spoken time:** dialogue Chinese characters ÷ 225 per minute, adjusted for cries, overlaps, ritual cadence, and deliberate pauses.
2. **Playable action:** individually timed tasks, travel within a set, prop handling, combat beats, and reactions. A sentence that only explains emotion contributes zero screen time.
3. **Transition allowance:** normally 3–8 seconds; no generic empty establishing shot may exceed 8 seconds without story information.
4. **Silence:** counted only when a person is withholding a specific answer while another person exploits that withholding.
5. **Action-heavy episodes:** EP05, EP09–11, EP14–16, EP18, EP26–28, and EP30 may carry 55–70% non-dialogue runtime.
6. **Performance-heavy episodes:** EP01–04, EP07–08, EP13, EP17, EP19, EP22–25, and EP29 may carry 50–65% dialogue runtime.

V2 target per episode:

- 5 scenes, normally 55–105 seconds each;
- 450–850 dialogue characters depending on action load;
- 1,300–2,100 total Chinese screenplay characters excluding metadata;
- at least three distinct physical state changes across the episode;
- no scene whose only event is information transfer.

## Binge rhythm lock

- EP01–04: compressed activation thriller; information always changes travel risk.
- EP05–08: release and hospitality; alternate physical survival with social scrutiny.
- EP09–15: retrospective descent; spectacle rises while command legitimacy falls.
- EP16–20: return compression; reduce geography, increase identity pressure.
- EP21–25: hall vise; each episode removes an exit, weapon, ally, or explanation.
- EP26–28: controlled explosion; action geography must remain legible.
- EP29–30: emotional and civic aftermath; do not imitate battle pacing.

Acceptance requires an estimated season runtime between 195 and 225 minutes, with no episode below 6:30 or above 7:30 under the documented method.
