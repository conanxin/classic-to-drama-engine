# 《归途：奥德修斯》V2 VFX and Creature Strategy

Status: `LOCKED_MEDIUM_COST_VFX_GRAMMAR`

The master screenplay chooses the MEDIUM option below. LOW is a contingency package; HIGH is an optional enhancement and may not alter source responsibility, performance timing, or the human choice already photographed.

| Element | LOW | MEDIUM — master | HIGH | Non-negotiable dramatic fact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cyclops | offscreen body, human shadow, giant hand/foot inserts, eye sound | forced-perspective performer, scaled cave pieces, practical hand/eye/ram units, limited comp | full digital body and environment interaction | Odysseus chooses to wait, invents Nobody, blinds, escapes, then exposes his name |
| Circe transformation | shadows, cups, sound, performers behind lattice | choreographed partial prosthetics, face/hand transitions, reflection morph, controlled animals | full-body digital transformations and multiple creature doubles | appetite enables capture; Odysseus resists; Circe restores the crew after oath |

## Additional divine/environment effects

- **Zeus:** practical pressure response, light, one controlled strike, breakaway deck. Voice is sparse.
- **Helios cattle:** silhouettes, branded badges, hide movement, meat sound, and one restrained practical/CG eye or breath detail.
- **Laestrygonian fleet loss:** one hero deck, scaled hull fragments, rock impacts, sound, and overhead plates; no twelve full digital ships required.
- **Phaeacian ship petrification:** locked practical hull fragment plus progressive texture comp; play witnesses and sound before the wide reveal.
- **Argos:** trained dog and controlled inserts; no digital emotional performance.

## Department handoff

Each effect receives a previsualized before/after plate, actor eye-line, physical contact point, sound premise, practical element, VFX limit, and editorial escape route. A VFX shot is rejected if it hides who made the consequential choice or breaks the established object geography.

Master result: `MEDIUM_COST`, 16 medium-or-higher VFX scenes and 8 creature-tagged scenes, with no required full-CG hero creature.
