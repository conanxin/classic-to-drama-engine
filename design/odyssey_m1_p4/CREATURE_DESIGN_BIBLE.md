# Creature and Mythic Presence Design Bible

Status: `FROZEN_P4_PRACTICAL_FIRST_CREATURE_SYSTEM`

`full-CG hero creature required: false`. Design begins with practical contact, in-camera scale or absence, limited VFX extension, sound, and actor reaction. A clean full-body monster beauty shot is not an acceptance target.

| System | Practical base | In-camera | VFX extension | Sound | Actor reaction / fail-soft cut |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cyclops | performer fragments, giant hand/foot/eye, scaled cup/rock/stake, oversized wool | forced perspective, low lens, foreground mismatch, shadow | brief size integration, rock path, eye cleanup | weight before image, cave air, single eye wetness | floor vibration and object displacement preserve scale if extension reduces |
| Circe transformation | hand/face prosthetic phases, practical penned animals | reflection and occlusion, controlled cup blocking | short face/limb transition only | human breath detunes into animal room tone | witnesses and changed hand function make result legible |
| Sirens | no clean bodies required; distant cloth/rock figures and mast restraint | heat shimmer, backlight, partial profile | limited distant separation/sea extension | seductive detail built from breath, rope and remembered voices | Odysseus' body against bonds is primary image |
| Charybdis | water tray, rope pull, rotating debris, hull tilt | lens close to surface, alternating horizon | contained whirl geometry and scale | low inhalation, timber stress, sound drop | crew balance and custody changes preserve threat |
| Scylla | six independent overhead rigs, partial neck/limb/maw fragments, six safe releases | silhouette intersections and foreground occlusion | brief limb/scale integration; no readable whole body | six spatially distinct attacks | eyeline targets, empty stations and bone counters carry cost |
| Underworld dead | practical ensemble, gauze, reflective floor, blood trench | layers, selective focus, movement rules | edge loss/body absence/reflection separation | voices occupy wrong depth | Odysseus controls blood access; contact failure stays practical |
| Poseidon pressure | partial raft/deck, motion, spray, rope and actor balance | horizon removal and moving source | environment extension only | sea precedes image, sub-bass without theme music | grip, stance and route loss lead spectacle |
| Athena transformation | matched human bodies/costume states, dust/cloth | crowd rhythm, reflected light, wrong-timed shadow | short matched blend and eye-light | ordinary sound misses one beat | humans still choose after access changes |
| Petrification | locked hull fragment with progressive practical texture | witness-led fixed wide and texture inserts | limited spread and water integration | timber resonance loses decay | Phaeacian witnesses, not a showcase morph, carry consequence |

P3's eight creature-classified scenes—EP09-S05, EP10-S01 through S05, EP12-S01 and EP14-S05—are explicitly covered by Cyclops/Circe/Scylla systems; the remaining systems cover the broader medium-VFX mythic grammar.
