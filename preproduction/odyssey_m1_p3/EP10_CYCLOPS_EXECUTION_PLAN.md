## Eye, blood and scale

- Eye unit versions: CLEAN, DILATED, CONTACT, BLOOD, POST. Each has lens-safe fluid and a numbered reference photograph.
- Blood begins only after stake contact. Cyclops face, stake, hand, stone and floor maps use the same take/state ID.
- The full creature is never required in close contact. Scale is carried by hand/foot/eye, cave pieces, reaction timing, sound and two limited silhouettes.
- Sheep are photographed as animals, not thrown or loaded. The human escape unit is a separate belly rig matched with wool inserts.
