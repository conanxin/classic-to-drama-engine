# Costume Design Bible

Status: `FROZEN_P4_COSTUME_VISUAL_SYSTEM`

Costume expresses access, work, travel, salt, ritual, wealth, mourning pressure, disguise, blood and repair. It is production clothing: seams permit movement, wet duplicates preserve weight, stunt duplicates preserve silhouette, and recognition details survive coverage.

## Principal construction rules

- Odysseus: one body identity under ten social conditions. `W-OD-05-CALYPSO-WORN` → `W-OD-05-RAFT-SALT` carries the same repair map; `W-OD-06-PHAEACIA-BORROWED` preserves Arete's blue stitch; `W-OD-17-26-BEGGAR` changes access with patch geometry and altered belt; `W-OD-27-BATTLE` adds B1–B5 blood overlays; EP29/30 clean without erasing seams or bruising.
- Penelope: old purple is a time-and-work ledger. Key clearance, loom sleeves and hem route precede beauty. The contest layer increases public authority; EP29 removes one outer layer without reset; EP30 physically joins old purple and undyed white.
- Telemachus: growth is fit, repair and custody. The early shoulder is wrong; travel belt is self-repaired; sword clearance and shield handling alter silhouette; civic state is correctly fitted and simpler, never a sudden hero cuirass.
- Athena: disguises use local fibers and tasks; owl-pin, hidden seam and gait line recur. Divine state is structural, not luminous fabric spectacle.

## Duplicate standard

Every principal action state has `HERO`, `WET`, `STUNT` and, where applicable, `BLOOD` duplicates. Visible stitch, buckle hole, hem length, dirt edge and material reflectance match. Wet state IDs are W0–W4; blood IDs B0–B5. A duplicate is approved only after a side-by-side 5600K and practical-light photograph.

## Ensemble grammar

- Ithaca household: earth/undyed vertical layers, key/tool clearance.
- Suitors: wine/rust, soft interior wealth, individual table-position identifiers.
- Phaeacia: sea-glass lateral drape, maintained repair.
- Sailors: tar/salt shoulder tabs tied to vessel identity.
- Underworld: color suppressed; death-specific edges and fabric physics remain distinct.
- Kin/civic: ash and family black marks moving toward undyed white, without uniformity.

Modern fashion-editorial tailoring, generic leather fantasy armor, spotless distressing and unexplained palette changes are rejected.
