# Publication Changelog Policy

## 1.0.0 — 2026-08-27

Initial frozen Digital Graphic Novel Edition: Web Comic, five volumes, PDF, EPUB 3 and CBZ.

## Version rules

- **patch (1.0.1):** metadata, typo or packaging correction that does not materially change story/art. Existing 1.0.0 binaries remain immutable.
- **minor (1.1.0):** new edition feature, additional export or accessibility enhancement with explicit successor manifests.
- **major (2.0.0):** material story, art, panel sequence or edition-architecture revision.
