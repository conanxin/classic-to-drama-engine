## Practical effects inventory

- Controlled fire: shielded hearth, pyre/fire bowl, Cyclops heat/smoke and sulfur basin. Flame size is the permitted practical, not an editorial implication.
- Breakaways: raft/mast/hull fragments, stool, tables, shields, selected spears and impact dressing. All have hero, rehearsal and camera units.
- Atmosphere: fog, dust, smoke, wind and rain are exposure-monitored. Atmosphere never hides an unsafe contact.
- Blood: Cyclops, voyage, hall characters and Underworld use distinct systems and cleanup. Blood tubing is pressure-tested before costume/HMU final.
- Motion: deck/raft bases have posted operating envelopes. Maximum wet, maximum tilt and overhead performer work are never combined without a separately approved cue stack.

## Rehearsal days

Target plan carries `7 dedicated rehearsal days`: 1 water/motion, 1 Cyclops, 1 Scylla/rig, 4 hall battle. Athletics, animal, low-contact and field top-ups occur inside prep/camera-tech windows and do not replace the dedicated days.

## Evidence at wrap

For every stunt scene: approved risk assessment, performer/double, prop version, rehearsal video, contact map, medic status, shot IDs completed, deviations, incident/near-miss field, and continuity handoff. A green take without this evidence does not close the scene.
