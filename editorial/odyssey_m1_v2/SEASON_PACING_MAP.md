## Macro waveform

1. **Rise (EP01–04):** household pressure turns into travel and ambush.
2. **Release/reversal (EP05–08):** sea survival opens into hospitality, then anonymity collapses.
3. **Descent (EP09–15):** external spectacle rises as command credibility and crew count fall.
4. **Compression (EP16–25):** sets contract around Ithaca while recognition evidence accumulates.
5. **Explosion (EP26–28):** three episodes share one spatial battle, each with a different tactical problem.
6. **Aftermath (EP29–30):** private and civic recognition complete return at lower spectacle but higher emotional consequence.

Hook diversity target: decision (EP01), threat (02/04/09/11/14), destination (03/06/12/16), identity question (07/18/20/22), revelation (08/19/23), moral loss (10/13/15), object transfer (24/25), violence (26), tactical reversal (27), relationship question (28/29), and conditional peace (30).
