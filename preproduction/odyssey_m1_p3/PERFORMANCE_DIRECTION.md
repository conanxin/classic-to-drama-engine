# Performance Direction

## Shared rule

Actors play a specific request, defense, concealment, test or task. They do not play “myth,” “recognition,” “pressure,” or a thematic summary. Stillness is active only when speech would surrender evidence, status, safety or intimacy.

## Principals

### Odysseus

- Rhythm: inventory, route and conditional clauses; wit is a tactical by-product.
- Physical center changes with identity: command weight in voyage, constrained access in Phaeacia, reduced footprint as beggar, exact economy in battle, exposed domestic awkwardness before Penelope/Laertes.
- Avoid: omniscient calm, a clever capstone every scene, heroic grief without accountability.
- Direction note: when he lies, identify the listener behavior he is trying to produce. When he tells truth, identify what control it costs.

### Penelope

- Rhythm: lets another person overstate, then asks for a measurable detail or changes custody.
- Her work—cloth, food, keys, accounts, witnesses, bow, bed—is the action line.
- Avoid: dreamy waiting, mystical intuition, coldness as a pose.
- Direction note: the bed test is not revenge. It is the smallest private false instruction that a real builder cannot accept.

### Telemachus

- Early voice breaks under interruption; he borrows objects/phrases before he earns procedure.
- Movement progresses from edge/chair/staff/reins/rowing to keys, shield, witness protection and a self-made staff.
- Avoid: adult hero cadence in EP01–04, imitation of Odysseus’ wit, uncomplicated battle pride.
- Direction note: after killing Amphinomus, effort to retrieve the spear matters more than the strike.

### Athena

- Each disguise belongs socially to the room but carries the same operational attention.
- She asks what a person will do and may change one condition; she does not supply the human answer.
- Avoid: generic serenity, magical exposition, visible satisfaction at manipulating mortals.
- Direction note: the aegis in EP28 is a one-beat condition after humans close the trap, not the winning move.

## Major supporting directions

- Antinous: possession language, appetite, blunt social certainty; little metaphor.
- Eurymachus: converts threat into terms, speaks faster when a deal is failing.
- Amphinomus: hesitation is real but does not erase participation; body repeatedly starts toward exit and returns.
- Eumaeus: portions, gates, animals and weather; affection is work performed without witness.
- Eurycleia: touch memory and household classification; emotion arrives through a task she cannot safely finish.
- Nausicaa: reputation and safety managed simultaneously; attraction never cancels calculation.
- Arete: forensic stillness; she identifies workmanship before accepting story.
- Circe: leisure is power; diagnoses appetite without rushing.
- Calypso: intimacy and possession coexist; anger redirects into sail/cloth/labor.
- Polyphemus: concrete ownership and bodily routine, not elegant monster speech.
- Laertes: land knowledge and manual memory; age limits sustained action but not one prepared choice.
- Poseidon: condition, duration and pressure; human-like debate is minimized.

## Crowd and ensemble

- Crowds are composed of foreground tactics, not a single mood. Assign 4–6 behavior cells per setup.
- Suitors have individually tracked appetites and escape strategies.
- Sailors accumulate grievance, exhaustion and casualty knowledge.
- Phaeacians watch hospitality become state risk.
- Kin in EP30 carry grief and evidentiary distrust, not anonymous revenge noise.

## Recognition performance

- Argos: Odysseus’ inability to acknowledge is the active cost; no sentimental dialogue.
- Scar: Eurycleia’s hand knows before her face; Odysseus’ restraint protects operation and harms intimacy.
- Bow: familiarity is heard and handled before proclaimed.
- Bed: Penelope controls prompting; Odysseus reacts as a maker whose shared work has been threatened.
- Orchard: Laertes rejects scar-only proof; bodily reunion arrives after land memory.

## Violence and safety performance

No actor is asked to sell danger inside a live weapon, water, animal, fire, height or rig path. Performance plates and contact plates are separated where required. Post-impact breath, balance, grip and decision carry consequence more effectively than harder contact.
