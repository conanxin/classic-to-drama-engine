# P7C Reader Research Plan

status: `READER_TEST_PREPARATION`
study type: product usability study, not a statistical-significance study
minimum useful sample: `6`
preferred sample: `10–15`
session length: `20–30 minutes`

## Research questions

1. Does Graphic Mode improve character recognition?
2. Does it improve relationship comprehension?
3. Does it make “who is doing what” easier to follow in a complex scene?
4. Is the reading experience easier to complete than Script Mode?
5. Does Graphic Mode create new visual or information overload?
6. Do character hints interrupt the story when they are too prominent?
7. Do images carry narrative work rather than decoration?
8. Do readers know when and how to open assistance?
9. Do readers intend to continue to the next episode?
10. Is the Script/Graphic dual-mode model clear?

## Cases

- **EP01 — onboarding:** new world, names, household roles and entry speed.
- **EP19 — identity/relationship:** reader knowledge versus scene knowledge, father/son verification and disguise.
- **EP27 — high-load action:** factions, doors, arrows, weapon custody and consequence.

## Conditions

Condition A reads EP01 in Script Mode and EP19 in Graphic Mode. Condition B reads EP01 in Graphic Mode and EP19 in Script Mode. Both read EP27 in Graphic Mode as the common high-load diagnostic. Participants never read the same episode twice, reducing immediate learning effects. Assignment alternates A/B in participant order.

## Participants

Recruitment is outside Codex authority and will be performed by the user if desired. The recommended mix includes people unfamiliar with *The Odyssey* and people familiar with it but new to this project. Developers may run technical checks, but they do not count as the entire reader sample.

## Evidence classes

- objective recall/comprehension answers;
- completion and elapsed task time;
- character, relationship and source-help openings;
- mode switch and next-episode intent;
- five short rating items;
- five optional open responses.

## Honesty boundary

Internal QA, model simulation and `SYNTHETIC TEST FIXTURE` data may validate the harness only. They never count as reader evidence. Until real exported participant JSON exists and is analyzed, the only valid decision status is `AWAIT_REAL_READER_EVIDENCE`.
