# Teaser Previs Concept — Who Is This Man When Nobody Recognizes Him?

Status: `PASS_P4_TEASER_CONCEPT_REALIZED`

This is a 78.4-second visual/rhythm proof, not a trailer and not a complete synopsis. It tests whether home, sea, storytelling, disguise, recognition objects, finite violence and civic return belong to one visual system.

## Dramatic spine

1. The house is occupied before the absent man is shown.
2. Telemachus leaves; sea pressure erases stable identity.
3. Odysseus speaks a name and images answer with consequences rather than triumph.
4. On Ithaca, dog, scar, bow and axes supply escalating but incomplete evidence.
5. Violence leaves residue; the bed and land demand standards public victory cannot satisfy.
6. Weapons lower and old purple joins undyed thread. Return becomes work.

## Edit grammar

- Sea is heard before the first image.
- Hard cuts dominate. Short dissolves are reserved for memory/absence and bed/land relations.
- Still-keyframe motion is limited to 3–6% push, pan or depth drift; no generic transition pack.
- Cyclops/Scylla remain fragmentary. Recognition images keep shared human frames.
- The first title card asks `当无人认出他，他是谁？`; the final card names `《归途：奥德修斯》` and `视觉预演 / P4`.

## Temporary original sound

No commercial music. The track is synthesized from sea-filtered noise, wood/rope creak, breath pulses, bow tension, twelve-axes ring, limited impacts and deliberate silence. It is an editorial timing tool, not final sound design.
