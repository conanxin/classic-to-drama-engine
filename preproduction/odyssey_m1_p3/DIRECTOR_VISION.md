# Director Vision — Home Must Recognize the Person Who Returns

Status: `FROZEN_DIRECTOR_VISION`

## Central proposition

《归途：奥德修斯》 is not a route home followed by a victory. It is a sequence of claims about who a person is, tested by people, objects, work and consequence. The world is mythically real, but a god cannot complete the human proof.

The season starts inside a house whose owner is absent but whose objects are being consumed. It travels through seas where Odysseus controls stories more easily than people, then returns to the house as a controlled stranger. Public competence ends the occupation; private knowledge and civic accountability make return possible.

## Audience experience

The viewer should constantly know more than one character and less than another. Suspense comes from unequal knowledge, not hidden information for its own sake:

- we see Telemachus learning before Penelope sees the result;
- we hear Odysseus’ voyage as a self-incriminating account before knowing which omissions his crew understood;
- we recognize the beggar before the household does;
- we witness scar and bow proof while Penelope still withholds recognition;
- we experience the bed test as a private standard no public victory can pass automatically.

## Eight directing axes

### Home

Home is a labor system—food, keys, cloth, doors, names, animals, stores and witnesses—not a warm establishing shot. Wide frames show who is doing work and who consumes it. A restored home keeps scars.

### Sea

The sea is heard and felt before it is shown. Balance, rope strain, wet cloth, horizon loss and an actor’s changing stance carry scale. Water is consequence; it is not travel wallpaper.

### Identity and disguise

Disguise changes access, not soul. We photograph what a character can touch, where they may sit and what name others use. A mask is successful until it prevents the relationship the character needs.

### Recognition

Recognition is an escalating standard of evidence. Shared frames maintain the social test; close images arrive only when a new kind of proof becomes available.

### Memory

Memory is not a neutral flashback. The Phaeacian audience remains present as pressure. Cuts into voyage material retain a physical trigger—a song, cup, scar, cloth, name or wound—and return with a changed listener.

### Violence

Violence consumes resources and relationships. Every hit changes space, custody, injury or judgment. It is legible and finite, never a generic rhythm bed.

### Return

Return compresses camera distance and geography while expanding the standard of proof. Odysseus sees home first as a stranger, then through loyal work, family doubt, household evidence, marriage knowledge, land and community.

### Gods

Divinity first appears as a condition: a crowd pauses, wind reverses, water enters architecture, light catches the wrong eye, a shield sound arrives. The camera finds the god only after showing what humans must still do.

## Ending promise

EP30 does not end with divine amnesia or a restored throne. Three generations lower weapons, a boundary stone is held from both sides, Penelope joins old purple thread to new undyed thread, and Telemachus cuts his own staff. Return is accepted as unfinished civic work.
